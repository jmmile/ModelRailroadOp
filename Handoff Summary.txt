### Current application structure

* Car Roster
* Industries
* Industry Tracks
* Car Locations
* Spot Occupancy

* Car History

### Current location system

Cars now use:

`Industry → Track → Spot`

The `Car` model contains:

* `industry_id`
* `track_id`
* `spot_id`
* legacy `location` text field

`CarLocationService` is the central service for:

* validating cars against spot restrictions
* assigning cars
* moving cars
* removing cars from locations
* finding eligible unassigned cars
* recording car movement history

### Important decision

Car types are intentionally specific. **Do NOT broadly normalize car types.**

Examples include:

* `Hopper - Grain`
* `Hopper - Coal`
* other specific identifiers

The spot assignment system uses exact car-type matching for restrictions.
